About KJS-CES-01 Portal

System Objective

The primary objective of the Heatwave Intelligence System is to deliver real-time thermal risk assessments and early warnings to municipal authorities and the public. By synthesizing atmospheric parameters, the system reduces heat-induced public health risks across monitored zones.

Data Sources & Ingestion

Our predictive platform ingests data streams from AWS ground sensors, regional IMD repositories, and geostationary thermal satellite feeds. Observations undergo automated anomaly filtering before updating the public bulletin.

Target Stakeholders

Designed to serve Disaster Management Officers dispatching local advisories, meteorologists analyzing thermal indices, and citizens seeking actionable health guidelines during extreme heatwaves.

Executive Leadership Message

"Timely climate intelligence is not merely a technical readout; it is an active public safety measure. Our mission with KJS-CES-01 is to bridge raw atmospheric data with immediate, life-saving civic response."
— Chief Meteorologist & Disaster Response Coordinator

System Data Sources & Stakeholder Hierarchy

Forecast & Alert Workflow Sequence

Automated pipeline followed by KJS-CES-01 from raw telemetry ingestion to emergency public dispatch:

  1. Continuous atmospheric observation and sensor data collection.
  2. Data ingestion, quality control, and heat-index calculation.
  3. Severity assessment against regional meteorological thresholds.
  4. Automated bulletin generation and alert verification.
  5. Multi-channel alert dissemination to civic authorities and citizens.

Primary Telemetry & Data Sources

Real-time climate streams integrated into our predictive platform:

  • Ground-level telemetry from terrestrial Automatic Weather Station (AWS) networks.
  • Historical temperature baseline datasets from the India Meteorological Department (IMD) database.
  • High-resolution thermal infrared feeds from geostationary satellite platforms.

Stakeholders & Operational Capabilities

Permitted actions and platform access levels mapped by user role:

  • Meteorologists & Technical Analysts
    • Override automated heat-index calculations when atmospheric anomalies occur.
    • Issue raw meteorological advisory bulletins and risk reports.
  • Disaster Management Officers
    • Trigger regional broadcast sirens and emergency broadcast SMS channels.
    • Coordinate municipal cooling center deployments and water supply distribution.
  • General Public & Citizens
    • View live hotspot risk overlays and city forecast tables.
    • Subscribe to location-based SMS and email warning notifications.

Technical Acronym Directory

AWS
Automatic Weather Station — An automated observation post equipped with digital instruments for measuring temperature, humidity, and atmospheric pressure.
IMD
India Meteorological Department — The principal government agency responsible for meteorological observations, weather forecasting, and seismology.
HW
Heatwave — A period of abnormally hot weather where maximum temperatures depart significantly from normal statistical baselines.
SHW
Severe Heatwave — An extreme heat event characterized by temperature departures exceeding normal baselines by 6.5°C or higher.
